A small guide to how notable websites look right now. Each family names what the eye notices first; each site gets one line on why it belongs there. Pick a family to narrow the list.

How this was made

One prompt to Claude Code asked for a static, single-page atlas: research public websites that stood out in 2025 and 2026, check every link in a browser, sort them into families with names and rules of our own, and write one line per site. Claude Code then wrote the HTML, CSS and a few lines of JavaScript, served the page locally and checked it at 375 and 1440 pixels.

There are no screenshots of the sites here, only links and our own words; the small pictures are CSS sketches of each family. The links were checked on 6 November 2026, and websites change, so a site may have moved on. Method after a post by @0xMovez on X.
